# Assets

Every asset bundled with or committed to EncoderCraft is listed here with its author, source and
license.

**Rule:** no Adobe or other proprietary icons, images, artwork or presets (`.epr`), ever. Assets
must be original, public domain (CC0) or permissively licensed. Fonts are not committed here: they
live in `storytold/craft-fonts`.

## Icons

| Path | Author | License |

| `packaging/macos/dmg/background.svg`, `background.tiff`, `dmg-layout.DS_Store` | the EncoderCraft contributors (generated by `generate.py`) | MIT OR Apache-2.0 |

The app icon is a placeholder until the owner draws the final one; see `assets/app-icon/README.md`.

## Font fallback

The UI embeds the default font manifest from [`storytold/craft-fonts`](https://github.com/storytold/craft-fonts/tree/aced4137c022f1f587624d48e81ec77e1914a9cc) at build time. Font binaries remain in that repository; the packaged application includes their attribution and SIL Open Font License 1.1 text in `NOTICE`.

| Build-time source | Authors | License |
|---|---|---|
| `storytold/craft-fonts/fonts/manifest.txt` (pinned build input; no font files are committed here) | Font authors listed in the pinned `craft-fonts/ATTRIBUTION.md` | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |
