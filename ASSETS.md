# Assets

Every asset bundled with or committed to EncoderCraft is listed here with its author, source and
license.

**Rule:** no Adobe or other proprietary icons, images, artwork or presets (`.epr`), ever. Assets
must be original, public domain (CC0) or permissively licensed. Fonts are not committed here: they
live in `storytold/craft-fonts`.
